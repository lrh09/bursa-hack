Method — built to avoid data-mining, not to flatter:
  • Universe: point-in-time top-K by trailing dollar-volume — a rule, no survivorship, no hand-picked coin list.
  • Window: the full 2018+ history (a multi-regime cycle: 2018 bear, 2020-21 bull, 2022 crash, 2023-25), not a cherry-picked slice.
  • Deflation: Deflated Sharpe Ratio computed over the entire 36-config parameter grid that was searched — plus PBO and CPCV out-of-sample.
Strategy: multi-speed time-series trend blended with cross-sectional momentum, optional absolute-trend (Faber) crash gate. Vol-targeted. All positions lagged one day (no look-ahead).

Honest caveats

  • A backtest is derived from the same data it is measured on. Only forward paper-trading is a true out-of-sample test.
  • ~10 years of history dominated by the 2020-21 bull; crypto drawdowns are deep and the asset class is young.
  • Shorting requires perpetual futures or CME crypto futures (MBT/MET) — not spot. Funding/borrow costs are not fully modelled.
  • Deflation covers this 36-config grid; the broader human search across the wider project is not fully captured, so the true multiple-testing penalty is somewhat larger.
  • Not investment advice. Research artifact only.
